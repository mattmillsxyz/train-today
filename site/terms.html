Safety comes first

Check with a parent before starting a new training plan. Warm up first, drink water, and stop straight away if something hurts.

DRILL is a training plan, not a coach, a physiotherapist or a doctor, and nothing in it is medical advice. It cannot see your athlete, does not know their injury history, fitness level or medical conditions, and cannot tell whether a movement is being done safely.

  • A parent or guardian should review the plan and supervise as they judge appropriate.
  • Talk to a doctor before starting if there is any injury, illness or medical condition, or any doubt at all about whether exercise is suitable.
  • Stop immediately on pain, dizziness, chest discomfort or unusual shortness of breath, and get medical help if it does not pass.
  • Skip anything that does not feel right. Every drill can be made easier, done for fewer reps, or left out entirely.
  • Use a safe space with good footing and room to move. Some drills involve sprinting and jumping.
