No account, login, or demo credentials are needed. The app opens straight
into a short setup and everything is immediately usable.

WHAT THIS APP IS
A training plan generator for young athletes, roughly ages 8 to 12. It
composes a session for each scheduled training day from a bundled library
of 39 exercises, then walks the athlete through each step with a timer and
spoken instructions.

GETTING STARTED
1. On first launch, tap "Let's go".
2. Enter any first name, or leave it blank and tap Next.
3. Pick one or more sports, then tap Next.
4. Accept the default training days (Mon to Fri) and session length, tap Next.
5. The reminder toggle is optional. Leave it off to skip the notification
   permission prompt, then tap Next.
6. Tap "Start training" to reach the Today tab.

WHERE TO FIND THE MAIN FEATURES
Today tab: the current day's session. Tap "Start" on any exercise to open
the step timer, which is the core feature. Tap the chevron on a row to read
the full written instructions. Tap the circle to mark an exercise done.
Saturday and Sunday are rest days by default. If you land on a rest day,
use the left arrow in the green header to reach a training day.

Calendar tab: month grid. Tap any day to open that day's session.
Progress tab: streak, totals, badges, and personal records.
Settings tab: change sports, days, session length, reminders, and audio.
Settings also has "Re-run setup" and "Delete all progress".

PRIVACY AND NETWORKING
The app makes no network requests of any kind. There are no accounts, no
analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. All data is stored
locally with SwiftData and UserDefaults. The privacy label is set to
"Data Not Collected" and that is enforced by the app having no networking
code at all.

NOTIFICATIONS
The optional daily reminder uses local notifications only, scheduled on
device with UNCalendarNotificationTrigger. There is no push server.

AUDIO
The step timer speaks each step using AVSpeechSynthesizer and plays short
synthesized cue tones. Both can be turned off in Settings under
"During a workout". The app ducks other audio rather than stopping it.

AGE RATING
Rated 4+. All content is original exercise instructions written for children.
There is no user generated content, no chat, no web view, and no external
links inside the app.
